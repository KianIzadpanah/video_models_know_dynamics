Provenance

Where every file came from, what each manifest records, and how to regenerate the run.

Manifests#

Each pipeline stage writes a manifest with one entry per clip. Together they give full provenance for all 81 clips.

Reproducing the run#

The pipeline is stage-by-stage and each stage is idempotent — a stage skips any clip whose manifest entry already matches its inputs. Re-running from scratch requires the MotionGPT3 checkpoint and the SMPL body models, neither of which is redistributable; both are referenced by path in configs/.

The clips on this site are transcodes

Everything shown in these pages is a web-sized H.264 copy — capped at 720 px wide, CRF 28, no audio, faststart — generated by the site's build.py. The full-resolution originals are the files listed above and were not modified. Frame counts, timings and playback rates are unchanged by the transcode; only spatial resolution and bitrate are.

The original contact sheet#

results/sheet.html is the standalone single-page contact sheet written during the run, before this site existed. It reads the same clips directly from data/ and remains the fastest way to see all 81 at full resolution on one page.
