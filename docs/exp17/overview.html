Exp 17 · Video model output as a condition for a motion model

Overview

Turn the pipeline around. Take a video the model generated, pull its pose out, keep five keyframes, and let a motion model fill in between them. Is that better 3D animation than the video's own pose?

complete2026-10-09Exp 15 videosGVHMRCondMDIin-betweening

The goal#

Every experiment so far ended by lifting the video's pose with GVHMR. This one asks whether a motion model can turn that into cleaner animation. It takes five keyframes from the lifted pose and lets CondMDI generate the motion between them. No new video is generated.

Two kinds of source video from Exp 15 are compared:

  • text-only video: Exp 15's text only, the prompt with no control.
  • skeleton-controlled video: Exp 15's hand · trunk, the full loop of MotionGPT3 → video model → motion model.

For each prompt, the best of the three seeds was used, with the same seed for both sources.

Setup#

  • Source: 12 prompts × 2 source videos = 24 motions, lifted with GVHMR.
  • Keyframes: five, evenly spaced (0·40·88·128·168), taken from the lift.
  • Motion model: CondMDI. Every result is fitted to SMPL and rendered on the neutral body.
methodwhat it does
GVHMR liftthe video's own pose on every frame — the baseline
lift, smoothedthe lift with light Savitzky–Golay smoothing (9 frames)
blenda straight line between the 5 keyframes
CondMDIthe motion model fills in between the 5 keyframes, no text
CondMDI + textthe same, plus the prompt

What came out#

What we observed
  • CondMDI is not cleaner by the numbers. Its foot skate is higher than the lift's (154 vs 122 mm/s) and its jerk is the same.
  • Light smoothing is cheaper and better. It cuts jerk from 106 to 28 and stays within 1 mm of the lift.
  • CondMDI's value is robustness. It looks natural on 24 of 24 motions against the lift's 22. It repairs broken lifts, though on climb_ladder it loses the climb itself. The blend is worst: natural on only 4 of 24.
  • Adding the prompt to CondMDI changes nothing. What the motion means is decided by the source video: the action is there on 9 of 12 text-only sources and 5 of 12 skeleton-controlled ones.
  • Best route: text-only video → GVHMR lift → light smoothing.
