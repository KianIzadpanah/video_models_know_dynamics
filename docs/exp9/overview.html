Exp 9 · Photoreal output, trajectory as control

Overview

Tell the model only where the body goes — a pelvis path, a few point tracks — and nothing about how it moves. The prompt and the video prior have to supply the action.

complete2026-09-15AMASS/BABELLTX-2.3 distilledMotion-Track IC-LoRApoint tracksGVHMR
seed
speed
Click any clip to open it frame-by-frame

The goal#

Experiments 7 and 8 gave the model a skeleton. This one gives it a trajectory: point tracks of the pelvis and, in some conditions, the hands and feet. The question is whether the model can produce the right gait from where the body travels, with no pose information.

There is no cheap interpolation baseline here, because a gait cannot be derived from a path. text only is the lower bound and the full skeleton is the upper bound.

Setup#

  • Input: the nine AMASS/BABEL clips from Exp 7, 169 frames @ 25 fps.
  • Control: point tracks drawn the way ComfyUI's LTXVDrawTracks draws them, on every frame.
  • Video model: LTX-2.3 22B distilled + Motion-Track-Control IC-LoRA (the skeleton column uses Union-Control), 768×768.
  • Recovery: GVHMR, re-rendered on a neutral SMPL body.
conditionwhat the model gets
text onlyno control — lower bound
root1 track: the pelvis
root + facing2 tracks: pelvis and a point 0.5 m ahead of it
root + limbs5 tracks: pelvis, both wrists, both ankles
skeletonfull skeleton on every frame — upper bound

What came out#

What we observed
  • Headline, run: the input is really a fast walk. Under skeleton the model walks, copying it faithfully. Under root it produces a real run: bent arms, airborne strides, a forward lean. That gait can only have come from the model's prior.
  • The track clearly reaches the model. Pelvis error drops from 1223 px with text only to 337 px with root.
  • Gains appear only where the pelvis moves. walk and jump improve. wave, throw and kick stay where they were.
  • Adding the facing point makes things worse, while the wrist and ankle tracks rescue the arc of throw.
